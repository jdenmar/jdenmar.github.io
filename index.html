Welcome to Oishii Chilli Oil
Homemade with love in Brisbane

Oishii Chilli Oil is a savoury, fragrant, and spicy condiment with layers of flavour in every spoonful. Made with a base of rice bran oil and fermented soybean paste, it delivers rich umami depth. The heat comes from green chillies — vibrant and zesty, not overpowering — balanced beautifully with the freshness of lemongrass, warmth of ginger, and the punch of garlic. Spring onion, salt, and pepper round it out for a well-balanced, aromatic chilli oil that’s both bold and addictive. It’s not just spicy — it’s complex, savoury, and fresh, perfect for drizzling, dipping, or mixing into your favourite dishes.

The Story Behind the Spice

Hi, my name is Jennifer and my partner is Andrew — the heart behind Oishii Chilli Oil. What started as a simple act of cooking for my family has blossomed into a small, family-run business born right here in Brisbane.

Oishii Chilli Oil is a unique and addictive blend made with only the finest natural ingredients — no preservatives, no additives, and absolutely no artificial flavours. Every jar is handmade in small batches to ensure maximum freshness and flavour.

Crafted with Purpose

Our chilli oil isn’t just delicious — it’s incredibly versatile. Whether you're drizzling it over dumplings, tossing it through noodles, or spicing up your eggs, Oishii Chilli Oil will take your favourite dishes to the next level.

Made With Love, Shared With You

We’re proud to share a little taste of our home with you. Thank you for supporting our small family business!
